s3log against s3-mem: lean client vs object_store

s3log-bench against the in-memory S3 server on one 2-core VM: closed-loop writers (each submits, waits for its result, repeats), 2 producers, 1 reader, 10 s per run, one run per point. Every run passed all protocol checks.

What it shows

  • With a 200 ms lag, the lean client reaches 8,763 commands/s at 4,000 writers; object_store levels off at about 2,750 from 1,000 writers on. Below 500 writers the two are the same: each writer waits about the lag for its result, so throughput is writers ÷ wait.
  • object_store was CPU-bound: the bench spends about 480 µs per command with it and 120–130 µs with the lean client. With object_store both processes used 1.6–1.8 of the 2 cores from 1,000 writers on; with the lean client that only happens at 4,000.
  • The lean client also cut median wait: at 4,000 writers and lag 200 ms, 417 ms against 1,356 ms.
  • At 4,000 writers the lean client hits the next limits: effects average 889 commands against a cap of 1,000, and bench plus s3-mem use 1.7 of the 2 cores.
  • With a 1,000 ms lag, the wait sets throughput: each writer manages about one command every 1.3–1.5 s, counting its slow submissions, until object_store runs short of CPU at 4,000 writers.
  • Unknown outcomes stay near 3%, the bench's injected slow writers, except object_store at 4,000 writers and lag 200 ms (7%).
